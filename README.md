# Hospitality Revenue Management & Business Analytics

## Project Overview

This project focuses on analysing hospitality revenue performance using a dataset based on GrandHorizon Hotels & Resorts Ltd. The objective is to explore hotel revenue patterns, examine relationships between business variables, perform statistical analysis and derive meaningful insights to support business decision-making.

## Work Performed

1. Data Preprocessing
- Identified and removed duplicate records.
- Checked and handled missing values.
- Examined data consistency and prepared a cleaned dataset for analysis.

2. Exploratory Data Analysis & Visualization
- Created charts to understand revenue trends and business performance.
- Prepared analytical tables to summarise important findings.
- Compared revenue performance across hotel classifications, destinations and financial quarters.

3. Statistical Analysis
- Performed descriptive statistical analysis.
- Conducted hypothesis testing, including a one-sample Z-test and paired sample t-test.
- Performed ANOVA analysis.

4. Correlation & Regression Analysis
- Developed a correlation matrix to examine relationships between business variables.
- Performed simple linear regression and multiple linear regression.
- Analysed the relationship between revenue-related variables and business performance.

5. Dashboard Creation
- Created dashboards to present key findings through visualizations, charts and analytical summaries.
- Used visual analysis to make business performance easier to understand.

## Tools & Skills Used

- Microsoft Excel
- Data Cleaning & Preprocessing
- Descriptive Statistics
- Data Visualization
- Hypothesis Testing
- Correlation Analysis
- Simple & Multiple Linear Regression
- Dashboard Creation
- Business Analytics

## Project Outcome

The project provided practical experience in transforming raw hospitality data into structured information, applying statistical techniques and presenting analytical findings through dashboards to understand revenue performance and business trends.
